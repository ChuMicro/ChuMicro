Schematics¶

The three figures in the buttons guide, generated rather than drawn by hand so the values printed on them and the arithmetic behind them cannot drift apart.

docs/img/ is regenerated by the docs build from support/docs/, so these live here instead, where git tracks them.

Regenerating¶

.venv/bin/python scripts/render_button_schematics.py

That writes all three. While working on one drawing, render just that one:

.venv/bin/python scripts/render_button_schematics.py --figure debounce-rc

schemdraw is a host-only documentation tool. It is installed in the repo .venv and belongs in no library's dependencies; nothing it produces reaches a device.

The figures¶

wiring-active-low.svg wiring-active-low Switch to ground, internal pull-up on Internal pull-up only
wiring-active-high.svg wiring-active-high Switch to 3V3 with an external pull-down R1 10 kΩ
debounce-rc.svg debounce-rc A capacitor at the pin, fed through a series resistor R1 10 kΩ, R2 2.2 kΩ, C1 2.2 µF

The RC values¶

Four constants at the top of the script produce every number printed on the RC drawing, so the labels and the arithmetic stay in step.

The capacitor refills through the pull-up and empties through the series resistor alone, so the two edges are never filtered equally: 4.8 ms on press against 27 ms on release. Sizing the press edge is what matters, because that is the edge a bouncing contact rides in on, and 2.2 µF puts it past the 1 to 5 ms a tactile switch spends settling.

The series resistor has to stay well under the pull-up. The library leaves the pin's own pull-up switched on, and R2 divides against it: at 2.2 kΩ against a 13 kΩ internal pull-up the pressed pin sits near 0.5 V, and at 10 kΩ it would sit near 1.4 V, which is not a valid low. That internal pull-up also parallels R1 while the capacitor refills, so a real board reaches a high sooner than the printed 27 ms. The drawing prints the slow end, which is the one to size a settle window against.

Buy a capacitor that still measures 2.2 µF at 3.3 V. A small ceramic loses much of its rated value under DC bias.

schemdraw notes¶

Two things that cost time when editing the script:

  • loc="top" and loc="bot" mean left and right on an element drawn downward.
  • .at() keeps the previous element's direction, so a Rect placed with it needs an explicit .right().
