## The problem (precise)

The skill's writer (`writers_wf.js`: 4 passes + per-symbol consolidation, multi-voice, body ALLOWED)
produces **correct but over-long / over-fact-bounded** comments. Three linked findings:
- **#8 over-fact-bounding** — the ledger over-produces facts (esp. on small files: ~12–13 facts on a
  23-line/4-symbol file), and every correct fact gets carried, forcing long descriptions. A small one-class
  file doesn't merit exhaustive minutiae even when correct.
- **#7 the independent summarizer reads CLEANER than the writer** — `summary.json` (one job, zero
  constraints) beats the generated docstrings (which juggle voice + discipline + ledger + format).
- **#6 same fact repeated across symbols** (largely mitigated by the dedup "state each fact once" rule, but
  watch it).

## Hard-won conclusions THIS session (do not relitigate)

- **The clean target style** = the committed `heartbeat.py` docstrings, generated by the registered agent
  `commenter-casual-friendly` (`.claude/agents/commenter-casual-friendly.md`) at commit `d139e882`, with
  `13a4a927` restoring 2 facts it had dropped. The user's "first"/clean `__init__` example is its output.
- **`commenter-casual-friendly` is NO-BODY + behavior-first.** Clean, but achieved cleanliness by **dropping
  facts** (that's what `13a4a927` restored). It had a ledger-free design.
- **NO-BODY IS INCOMPATIBLE with our ledger.** We carry must-carry facts; force no-body and the writer must
  either CRAM (comma-heavy run-ons) or DROP (violate the ledger). The body is the release valve. REJECTED.
- **The clean heartbeat was clean because of LOW FACT COUNT per symbol** (cull + altitude moved cross-cutting
  facts up), NOT because body was banned. No-body happened naturally when there was little to say.
- **So the lever is FACT COUNT, not the body.** Want: a **CONTROLLED body** — kept, bounded, the writer aims
  tight even when the voice may use more words. Voice adding words is fine; we lost *discipline/tightness*.
- **Do NOT over-harvest from `commenter-casual-friendly`.** The new approach is a different idea on purpose;
  grafting its discipline wholesale may reintroduce the voice issues we fixed. Pull little, carefully.
  Ledger-AGNOSTIC wins worth considering (cautiously, behind tests): behavior-first/verb-led summaries; fold
  a contract detail into `Returns:`/`Raises:` when it fits there; its anti-pattern ban-lists + failure-mode
  few-shots. NOT its no-body rule.

## The levers (for the exp rounds)

1. **Cull low-vitality facts** (#8). Load-bearing (correctness-critical: inversion / referent / dual-role /
   boundary; dangerous traps: data-loss / silent-wrong; non-derivable domain) = ALWAYS carried. Minor
   (derivable-at-a-glance / low-impact) = voice may drop for narrative. Conservative default. Validator floor
   keeps correctness-critical facts; report surfaces a dropped-list (never silent).
2. **Altitude** (#6). Cross-cutting contracts stated ONCE at the highest relevant symbol; methods carry only
   local facts, don't restate class/module facts.
3. **Controlled body** (#7). Body kept but earned + tight; aim short even when allowed more.
