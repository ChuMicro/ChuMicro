- [ ] **workbench/deploy CircuitpythonTransport tests — eliminate real `subprocess.run(["sync"])` calls** (filed 2026-05-12 during preflight-timing investigation) — six tests in `test_circuitpython_transport.py` (`test_waits_for_board_to_see_new_entrypoint`, `test_post_rsync_verification_failure_raises`, four `TestWipeFilesystem::test_flash_mode_*` cases) collectively cost **~80 s of wall-clock time** out of preflight's 73-s `test` phase. This is the single biggest preflight-speed lever in the workspace today.
